Before We Knew

My love,

Sometimes I think about us and wonder how we even got here.

Because if someone had told me in 2023 that the funny username I kept noticing in the youth group chat would one day become my boyfriend, I honestly would have laughed. 😂

Before we actually met, I already knew of you.

You were just a username in the youth group, but I noticed your messages because you were genuinely funny. I’d see something you’d say and think,

“This guy is actually really fun. I think I’d really like to be his friend someday.”

And that’s honestly all I thought.

A friend.

Nothing more.

Then came the wedding.

Our first proper meeting, and somehow we just clicked. I felt so comfortable around you, and I remember thinking,

“There’s absolutely no way this guy is ever going to be my boyfriend.”

Look at us now. 😭❤️

And then, embarrassingly enough, I texted you first. 😂

You said something hilarious in the group, and I took my little opportunity to reply privately.

And somehow, that one message became conversations.

The conversations became long calls.

And the long calls became a normal part of our lives.

You’d be at work using the landline, sometimes your phone, and we’d just talk.

For so long.

To the point where my family started wondering,

“Who is this guy you are always talking to?” 😂

We became friends so naturally.

We went to concerts together—you were actually the one who introduced me to concerts. 😂

Our first one was A Night of Thanksgiving on 1 January 2025, and I don’t think either of us knew then how many memories we’d eventually have together.

Then came the airport.

You were leaving for the UK.

I remember being there and wanting to cry, but I was way too embarrassed to actually cry. 😂

And then you left.

Normally, that might have been the point where the story ended.

But somehow, ours didn’t.

We stayed in touch.

We remained friends.

And eventually, our friends and family started noticing something.

They started asking questions and making comments that made it seem like they thought there was something more between us.

And in my head, I would always think,

“No, we’re just friends.”

Because that’s genuinely what I believed we were at the time.

Until somewhere along the way, I started feeling uncomfortable with this whole “friendship without borders” situation.

So I asked you what you thought we were.

And you told me you thought we were just friends.

I won’t lie, Moyo Ndizvo…

That one hurt.

Probably more than I wanted to admit at the time.

But looking back now, I’m actually grateful for that moment.

Because maybe God was protecting our story.

Maybe we were trying to rush something He wasn’t finished writing.

Maybe we needed that pause.

That setback.

That space.

We needed time to grow individually and understand what we actually wanted.

So we continued as friends.

Things became a little quieter.

The long calls weren’t as frequent.

The boundaries became clearer.

And although I didn’t understand it then, maybe that was exactly what we needed.

Then, about a year later…

You asked me to be your girlfriend.

And suddenly, after all those years of friendship, conversations, distance, confusion, and waiting…

There we were.

You and me.

For real. ❤️

And you know what makes me laugh?

I didn’t even say yes immediately.

You gave me time to pray about it and think about it.

And I took that seriously.

I prayed.

I thought.

I asked God.

And eventually…

I said yes.

And honestly, babe, I’m so glad I did.

Because now we’re here.

Still long distance.

Still figuring things out.

Still learning each other in this new way.

Still having long conversations.

Still laughing.

Still annoying each other. 😂

Still choosing each other.

And most importantly, still trusting that God is in this.

We still have so much ahead of us.

There are promises we’re waiting to see fulfilled.

There are places we’ll go.

Things we’ll accomplish.

Prayers we’ll watch God answer.

And hopefully a whole lot more memories we’ll make together.

And yes, Mr M, there’s still that sofa conference we need to make happen. 😂❤️

But I know we’ll get there.

Because you’re a man of your word.

You keep your promises.

And that’s one of the things I admire about you.

So today, on your 28th birthday, I just wanted to remind you of where we came from.

From a username in a youth group chat…

To a random wedding…

To me embarrassingly texting you first…

To landline calls at work…

To concerts…

To an airport goodbye…

To the UK…

To “we’re just friends”…

To a whole lot of confusion…

To prayer…

To finally becoming us.

And honestly?

What a journey. ❤️

I wouldn’t rewrite a single part of it.

Because every awkward, confusing, funny, painful and beautiful moment brought us here.

And maybe that’s the most beautiful part of our story:

God was writing it long before we knew what the story was.

A Letter From Future Us

My love,

If you’re reading this, then somehow five years have passed.

And I wonder if the two of us sitting here, reading these words together, would believe how much life has happened since your 28th birthday when Joice wrote them.

I hope we’re smiling.

I hope we’re sitting somewhere that feels like home, looking at each other and thinking:

“We really made it.” ❤️

Five years ago, I wrote this as a prayer because I didn’t know exactly what our future would look like.

But I knew what I hoped for.

I prayed that our love would grow deeper—not just through the beautiful moments, but through every season that would teach us how to love each other better.

I prayed that we would still be friends.

That even after years of knowing each other, we’d still laugh at the stupidest things, have ridiculously long conversations and genuinely enjoy being around each other.

I prayed that the distance that once separated us would become nothing more than a story we tell.

That we would no longer have to count down the days until the next visit because we’d finally be able to wake up beside each other.

I prayed that we would build a home together.

Not just a beautiful house, but a home filled with peace, laughter, prayer, warmth and God.

I prayed for our careers and our dreams.

That you, Mr M, would look back at the man you were at 28 and be proud of how far you’ve come.

That the ambitions you carried in your heart would have become accomplishments you could see, touch and celebrate.

I prayed that my own dreams would also have flourished, and that we’d always support each other’s growth.

That your wins would feel like my wins, and mine would feel like yours.

I prayed for financial stability—not just money, but wisdom, discipline and the ability to build something meaningful together.

I prayed that we’d travel.

That we’d collect places, photographs, stories and memories that we could never have imagined when we were doing long distance.

I prayed for family.

For healthy relationships, full tables, loud laughter, birthdays, Christmases and ordinary days that somehow become our favourite memories.

And maybe, if God has blessed us that way, little feet running around our home someday. ❤️

I prayed that if we became parents, we’d raise children who know what love looks like because they watched their parents love, respect, forgive, pray for and choose each other.

I prayed that we’d never stop praying together.

That when life became difficult, we’d run towards God together rather than away from each other.

I prayed for health, protection, provision and opportunities we couldn’t have planned ourselves.

I prayed that God would open doors only He could open.

And most importantly, I prayed that whatever accomplishments we achieved, we’d never forget that the greatest blessing wasn’t what we owned, where we lived, what we drove or what was written next to our names.

It would be us.

Five years ago, I didn’t know where we’d live.

I didn’t know what we’d achieve.

I didn’t know what challenges we’d face.

But I knew one thing.

I wanted to choose you.

And my prayer was that five years later, I’d still be able to say the same thing.

So, future us, I hope we’re proud.

I hope we can look back at the two people who started as friends, who slowly became something more, who navigated distance, uncertainty, growing pains and everything in between—and tell them:

“You did well. You kept choosing each other.”

Maybe everything we prayed for hasn’t happened yet.

And that’s okay.

Because perhaps the greatest accomplishment isn’t getting everything we asked God for.

Perhaps it’s having God, each other and dreams still worth praying for.

So here’s my prayer for us:

May God continue to bless our relationship.

May He protect what we’ve built.

May He give us wisdom in every decision we make together.

May He bless the work of our hands.

May He enlarge our capacity to love, forgive and understand each other.

May He give us a home filled with peace.

May He bless our families.

May He provide for us beyond what we could ask or imagine.

May He guide our careers and our dreams.

May He bless us with beautiful experiences and meaningful memories.

May He give us grace for every difficult season.

And may He keep our hearts anchored in Him as we build the life we’ve always prayed for.

And five years from now, when we read this again, I hope we look at each other, smile, hold hands and say:

“Look at what God has done.”

Keep dreaming.

Keep becoming the man you’re meant to be.

Here’s to our future.
